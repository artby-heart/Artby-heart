Our Story

Crafted by Heart,
Styled for You

Hi, I’m Khushi 💅✨

By profession,I work as a robotics engineer in this corporate job,but my heart has always belonged to art, creativity, and colors, even if my day job is more about machines and tech.

Since early 2026, I started getting into nail art, I love creating all kinds of nail designs—from elegant bridal nails to simple and classy casual nails, and trendy Korean-style nails. Every set I design is made with care, creativity, and a personal touch.

I experiment a lot with techniques too, like gel or acrylic, and adding those detailed designs. Sometimes I push myself to try a new pattern or color mix, and it might not always turn out perfect, but thats part of it. My robotics side brings in this precision that helps get things exact, while the painting part I love adds the creative spark. It seems like that mix makes my work stand out a bit, full of energy or something.This combination makes my nail art different and full of life.

I truly love what I do, and my goal is to make every client feel confident, happy, and beautiful with their nails 💖

Let’s create something amazing together!
